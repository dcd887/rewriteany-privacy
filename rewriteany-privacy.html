RewriteAny Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

RewriteAny ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our browser extension.

1. Information We Collect

RewriteAny operates on a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model. This means:

  • Your text content never leaves your browser. When you select text and request a rewrite, the selected text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider's API using your own API key.
  • Your API key is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your API key, nor can we transmit it to any third-party server.
  • No usage analytics or telemetry are collected. We do not track which text you select, what rewrites you request, or any browsing activity.
  • The only data stored locally includes your AI provider selection, API key, and token usage statistics — all within your browser's local storage.

2. How Your Information Is Used

  • Text rewriting: Selected text is sent to the AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini) via their official APIs for processing. This occurs entirely between your browser and the AI provider.
  • API key storage: Your API key is stored in your browser's local storage. It is never transmitted to RewriteAny servers.
  • Token tracking: We estimate token usage to help you monitor your API costs. This data stays on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

RewriteAny does not operate its own servers. The only third-party services involved are:

  • AI API Providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Gemini. You interact with these services directly using your own accounts and API keys. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.
  • Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store: Store operations and policy enforcement. We do not have access to your store account data beyond what the store provides for extension management.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • API keys are stored locally in your browser's secure storage.
  • All AI API calls use HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • No data is transmitted to RewriteAny-owned servers.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

RewriteAny is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated through the extension's update notes. Your continued use of RewriteAny after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hq15012670635@163.com or through the extension's settings page.

This policy applies to the RewriteAny browser extension only and does not cover third-party websites or services you may visit.
