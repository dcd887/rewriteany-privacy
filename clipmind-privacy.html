ClipMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ClipMind ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our browser extension.

1. Data We Collect

ClipMind operates entirely on your device. Specifically:

  • Your clipboard history is stored locally only. All copied text is saved in your browser's local storage and never transmitted to any server.
  • No personal data is collected. We do not collect your name, email, IP address, or browsing history.
  • No analytics or telemetry. We do not track which websites you visit, what text you copy, or how you use the extension.
  • Subscription status is stored locally. Your Pro subscription flag is kept in your browser's local storage and is not reported to any third-party server.

2. How Your Data Is Used

  • Clipboard management: Copied text is stored locally to provide clipboard history functionality within the extension popup.
  • Search and filtering: Search is performed entirely client-side using your local clipboard data.
  • Export / import: You can export your clipboard history as a JSON file and import it back — all data stays on your device.
  • Pin items: Pinned items are stored in your browser's local storage.

3. Third-Party Services

ClipMind uses the following third-party services:

  • Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store / Chrome Web Store: For extension distribution and policy enforcement. We do not have access to your store account data beyond what the store provides for extension management.
  • Creem: Used only for processing subscription payments. When you subscribe, payment information is handled entirely by Creem's secure payment page. We do not store or access your payment details. Only a local boolean flag indicating Pro status is saved in your browser.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Used to relay subscription status checks between the Extension and Creem. Only your anonymous device identifier and optional email address are transmitted for the purpose of verifying your subscription.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All clipboard data is stored locally in your browser — no data leaves your device.
  • Payment processing is handled by Creem using industry-standard encryption.
  • No clipboard data is ever transmitted to our servers. Only anonymous subscription status is exchanged.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

ClipMind is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated through the extension's update notes. Your continued use of ClipMind after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hq15012670635@163.com or through the extension's settings page.

This policy applies to the ClipMind browser extension only and does not cover third-party websites or services you may visit.
